A little structure. A better practice.

  1. Pick your chords. Choose two or more guitar chords and set how long you want to spend on each pair.
  2. Start switching. Alternate between the two chord shapes shown, at your own pace. Numbers on the dots indicate fingers; ○ means an open string and × means don’t play it. Strings run from low E on the left to high e on the right.
  3. Follow the flow. The timer moves to the next pair automatically. Every unique pair appears once, in a random order. A ↔ C includes practice in both directions.

Pause anytime with the space bar. Turn on sound for a gentle chime when each new pair starts and a brighter one when the session is complete. On a phone, turn on keep-awake so the screen doesn’t dim while you play.
